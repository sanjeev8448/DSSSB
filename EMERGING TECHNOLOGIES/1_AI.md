## 1. Artificial Intelligence (AI)

Definition: Artificial Intelligence is a branch of computer science that develops systems capable of performing tasks that typically require human intelligence, such as learning, reasoning, problem-solving, perception, and understanding language.

In simple words, AI enables computers to perform tasks that appear intelligent.

### Main capabilities of AI

- Learning: Improving performance from data or experience.
    
- Reasoning: Drawing conclusions from available information.
    
- Problem-solving: Finding solutions to a problem.
    
- Perception: Interpreting images, sounds, and other inputs.
    
- Natural Language Processing (NLP): Processing and generating human language.

## 3. AI vs Machine Learning (ML)

Machine Learning is a subset of Artificial Intelligence. It enables computers to learn patterns from data and use those patterns to make predictions or decisions, rather than relying only on explicitly programmed rules.

## Artificial Intelligence (AI)

The broader field of intelligent computer systems

### Machine Learning (ML)

Learns patterns from data

Deep Learning (DL)

Uses multilayer neural networks

### Relationship Between AI, ML, and Deep Learning

- **Artificial Intelligence (AI):** The broader field of intelligent computer systems.
    
- **Machine Learning (ML):** A subset of AI that learns patterns from data.
    
- **Deep Learning (DL):** A subset of ML that uses multilayer neural networks.

Relationship: Deep Learning ⊂ Machine Learning ⊂ Artificial Intelligence.

### Key differences

|Basis|Artificial Intelligence|Machine Learning|
|---|---|---|
|Meaning|Broad field of intelligent systems|Subfield of AI focused on learning from data|
|Approach|May use rules, search, logic, ML, or other methods|Learns patterns from data|
|Data requirement|Depends on the technique|Training data is generally central|
|Goal|Perform tasks requiring intelligent behavior|Learn patterns to predict or make decisions|
|Example|Rule-based expert system|Spam email classifier trained on examples|

### Example: Email spam detection

- AI: The overall goal is to identify and filter spam emails.
    
- Rule-based AI: A system flags emails containing certain predefined suspicious words or patterns.
    
- ML: A model learns patterns from labelled spam and non-spam emails to classify new messages.
    

This example shows that AI can use machine learning, but AI does not always require ML.

### Related terms to remember

- Deep Learning (DL): A type of ML that uses neural networks with multiple layers.
    
- Natural Language Processing (NLP): Technology for processing and generating human language.
    
- Computer Vision: Technology that enables computers to interpret images and videos.
    
- Generative AI: AI that creates content such as text, images, audio, or code.
