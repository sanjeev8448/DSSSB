## 1. What is Big Data?

Big Data refers to datasets that are so large, rapidly generated, diverse, or complex that traditional data-processing methods may not be sufficient to store, process, and analyze them efficiently.

Big Data technologies help organizations collect, store, process, and analyze such datasets to discover patterns and support decisions.

Examples include social media activity, online shopping transactions, sensor readings, and traffic data.

## 2. The 5 Vs of Big Data

1. Volume — Amount of data

Refers to the enormous quantity of data generated and stored.

Example: A social media platform stores billions of posts, comments, photos, and videos.

Keyword: How much data?

2. Velocity — Speed of data

Refers to the speed at which data is generated, received, processed, and sometimes analyzed in real time.

Example: A payment system processes transactions as they occur to identify suspicious activity.

Keyword: How fast?

3. Variety — Different forms of data

Refers to the different types, formats, and sources of data.

- Structured: relational database tables
    
- Semi-structured: JSON and XML
    
- Unstructured: images, audio, video, and free-form text
    

Example: An e-commerce company analyzes tables, customer reviews, and product images.

Keyword: What types of data?

4. Veracity — Data quality and trustworthiness

Refers to the accuracy, reliability, consistency, and uncertainty of data.

Example: A company checks whether customer records contain duplicates, incorrect addresses, or misleading entries.

Keyword: Can the data be trusted?

5. Value — Useful insights

Refers to the meaningful benefits or insights obtained by analyzing data.

Example: A retailer analyzes sales data to improve inventory planning and product recommendations.

Keyword: What benefit does the data provide?

## 3. Quick comparison of the 5 Vs

|V|Meaning|Example|
|---|---|---|
|Volume|Quantity|Billions of stored records|
|Velocity|Speed|Real-time sensor readings|
|Variety|Different formats|Text, images, JSON, video|
|Veracity|Trustworthiness|Detecting inaccurate records|
|Value|Useful benefit|Improving business decisions|
